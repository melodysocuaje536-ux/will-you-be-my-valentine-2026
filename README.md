## 💌 What Is This?

This is the **cutest Valentine's Day proposal code** on the internet — a fully interactive, **HTML5 CSS3 JS project** wrapped in the dreamy **Coquette Aesthetic** that's taking over TikTok in 2026.

Send it to your crush, your partner, or your pookie — and watch them try (and fail) to say no. 😏

### ✨ Why it goes viral:

> The "No" button **never works**. Every time they click it, the text changes to something more desperate & adorable — and the "Yes" button **grows bigger and bigger** until it's impossible to ignore. It's giving unhinged romance and the internet *lives* for it.

Think: **Gen Z Scrapbook Style** love letter meets chaotic boyfriend energy. It's the **TikTok Viral Website Idea** you didn't know you needed.

## 🚀 How to Use (Even If You've Never Coded Before)

### Step 1: Clone the Repo
```bash
git clone https://github.com/Yourfiyan/will-you-be-my-valentine-2026.git
cd will-you-be-my-valentine-2026
```

### Step 2: Make It Yours 🎨
- **Swap the images:** Replace `waiting.webp` (the "waiting for answer" image) and `yes.webp` (the celebration image) with your own cute photos or GIFs of you & your person!
- **Edit the messages:** Open `script.js` and change the `messages` array to your own guilt-trip lines.
- **Change the heading:** Open `index.html` and change `"Hey You!!"` to your crush's name or nickname.

### Step 3: Send It! 💕
- Open `index.html` in your browser to preview.
- Deploy it for free on **GitHub Pages**, **Netlify**, or **Vercel**.
- Send the link to your crush and wait for the magic. ✨

> **Pro Tip:** Host it on GitHub Pages for free! Go to your repo → Settings → Pages → Deploy from main branch. Done. 🎀
